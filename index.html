Details & use conditions

893 retargeted reference trajectories: every clip that passed the released NVIDIA SONIC G1 controller in nominal MuJoCo simulation. Some contain brief self-collision or foot sliding; per-clip metrics and a stricter subset (123 clips) are in the dataset. This is not hardware certification or a frame-by-frame manual review.

Reference (default) shows the retargeted target motion. SONIC shows the saved controller simulation tracking that target. They are different trajectories; neither is a physical robot recording. No original AIST video, music or SMPL annotations are included.

Source: AIST Dance Video Database / AIST++. Retargeting: PhySINK / PHUMA. Simulation: NVIDIA SONIC in MuJoCo. Robot: Unitree G1. Users and downstream redistributors must cite Tsuchida et al., AIST Dance Video Database, ISMIR 2019. Commercial use is subject to the AIST Dance DB Terms of Use.

About this comparison

Reference is the retargeted target; SONIC is the saved controller simulation. Each view follows its own robot root, so relative placement is not a shared world-space camera or drift comparison. The reference NPZ is the target motion, not a low-level robot command.
